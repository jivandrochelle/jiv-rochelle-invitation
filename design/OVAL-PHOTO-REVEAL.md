# Oval photo reveal

The gallery has one brush reveal, framed by the supplied floral oval. The photograph and fine sand cover share a clipped elliptical opening beneath the transparent frame. `src/sandReveal.js` draws warm granular sand on canvas; brushing clears a soft, irregular trail, pushes soft granular ridges to its sides, and scatters sand in the stroke direction. Faster sweeps send more grains outward; larger grains have small highlights and slow down before settling. A separate layer leaves a denser dusting of slightly larger, translucent sand grains on brushed areas until it fades away with the final reveal; repeated brushing cannot erase this last dusting. Particle animation is capped at 180 grains and 36 short-lived ridge patches, stops after they settle, and clears when offscreen, hidden or unmounted. Reduced-motion preferences preserve brushing without flying grains. Coverage samples the same brush opacity at visible oval cells, including partly swept edges; hidden corners cannot trigger completion. After 55% of the visible sand is cleared, the remaining cover fades gently over 2.6 seconds with a soft start and finish. The Reveal button uses the same fade; reduced-motion preferences retain an immediate reveal. Reveal and Cover buttons remain available. Both left and right drags advance the five-photo album to the next memory, with the outgoing card following the drag direction. Short drags snap back, and the album loops after the last photo.

Five prepared petal layers drift diagonally across the oval opening, in front of the photo, lettering and brush veil, below the frame ribbon. They use the softened slender, rounded and curled assets, sized another 5% smaller (5.7–7% of the opening width). Like the second image, rolling releases at 1, 10, 22, 31 and 43 seconds of a 52-second cycle overlap around mid-flight. Uneven 9–12-second intervals produce two or three visible petals after startup instead of separate waves. Flights last about 25 seconds, with varied flutter, angles and wind paths. Five-cycle server-rendered checks verify the overlap and absence of wave gaps. The layer ignores pointer events so brushing and touch scrolling remain available, pauses offscreen and in hidden tabs, and hides for reduced motion and print. No new artwork was generated; see SOFTENED-HERO-PETALS.json for the reused asset paths and prompts.

The reveal uses `public/photos/the-proposal.webp`, converted from the supplied `The proposal (replace law school days).jpg`. Its horizontal focal point is 27%, balancing the couple's clearance from the left and right floral borders. The five album photos match the supplied filenames: Us, always; Little moments; Food trips; Adventures; and A favorite memory. Little moments uses a full landscape view against linen to preserve both faces. The supplied originals remain in `src/assets/Pics for wedding invitation/`; browser-ready copies in `public/photos/` are orientation-corrected, converted to sRGB, and resized to a maximum 1600-pixel edge.

## Verification

The sand and gallery tests verify soft brush opacity, continuous strokes, progress within the oval, repeated brushing of one patch, settling particles, offscreen cleanup and reduced-motion behavior. A native canvas still render compares the coverage estimate with actual erased pixels (24.86% estimated versus 25.15% rendered for the checked stroke sequence), and shows the intact cover, partly brushed image and final photo under the real frame. Browser automation is unavailable, so live mouse and touch visual QA remains unverified.
