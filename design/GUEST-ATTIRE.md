# Interactive wedding guests

The Dressed for love section displays six embroidered guests from the supplied `src/assets/colothes model` folders. All ten blue and pink swatches are available to everyone. Selecting a shade changes all six guests and marks only that swatch as selected. The original mixed Misty Blue and Blush Pink preview appears before a visitor makes a choice. The page explicitly says everyone may wear either color family.

The supplied images include five blue shirt/tie variants per man and five pink dress variants per woman. [ATTIRE-ASSETS.json](ATTIRE-ASSETS.json) maps all 40 original PNGs to optimized runtime WebP files in `src/assets/attire-guests/`. Originals remain preserved. Runtime versions preserve transparency, use height 960 and quality 90. The active six-person lineup uses 30 variants. The similar right-male-2 and left-female-2 figures are omitted from the lineup and runtime bundle. Each person's original variants remain mapped by garment color; the main woman's pink order differs from filename order.

Blue dresses and pink shirts/ties use a code-based SVG color treatment of the first-shade portraits. `src/attireRecolor.js` defines garment boundaries and color curves; `GuestOutfit.jsx` applies them only to the clothing. Each dress has detailed traced neckline, sleeve opening, arm, hand, jewelry, bag and wrap boundaries, including the thin shoulder straps. Dresses recolor the complete fabric area so the original pink cannot remain between warm stitches. The men's full shirt/tie contours in `src/attireFabricContours.js` follow the original first-shade garment pixels, including collar folds, the shirt hem above the belt, cuffs and cool gray border threads. The previous runtime blue-color key was removed because it skipped gray fabric. The source fabric is neutralized before applying the chosen swatch, preserving dark stitching and white highlights. No original bitmaps or poses are changed, and no additional raster downloads are needed.

The SVG filter reads the inline vector garment mask and adds complementary clothing/detail regions before drawing the portrait once. Their alpha sums to the original source alpha; translucent thread edges no longer get darker or more opaque from two copies of the figure. The 2026-11-09 cleanup was reviewed in all 60 guest/shade combinations, with separate five-blue-shade close-ups for each woman in `output/attire-mask-review/`. Regression points cover the previously missed neckline, wrist and inner-arm fabric, along with protected skin, jewelry, bags and wrap.

Skin protection also checks softly averaged original colors in limited arm/hand/neck regions. Separate color checks distinguish skin from the original pink dresses and blue shirts/ties. A small opening operation removes isolated warm fabric stitches from that classification. The original skin and translucent arm-edge pixels are excluded from recoloring even when they extend beyond a traced polygon. Women's guards stop below the sleeve openings; men's guards cover only the hands, cuffs and neck so neutral shirt threads remain part of the selected color treatment. Enlarged original-versus-updated skin comparisons for every guest are saved in `output/attire-mask-review/`.

The user's marked tie, collar, waistband, three sleeve openings, shoulder seam and narrow strap were compared at five times their source size in `output/attire-marked-review/`. Local vector tracing used a 3-by-3 alpha-weighted blue-minus-red mean above 1.5, source alpha at least 225, garment seeds, enclosed-highlight filling, one-pixel recovery of neighboring cool border threads and contour simplification within half a source pixel. The source files are `right-male-1-1.webp`, `left-male-1-1.webp` and `left-male-2-1.webp`; the original assets remain preserved. Marked fabric and adjacent skin/belt regression points cover these corrections.

Fine pink fabric strips beside the crossed hands, right forearm and handbag bottoms are corrected by the compound vector paths in `src/attireCutoutRepairs.js`. These corrections apply only inside the existing detail cutouts and four marked rectangles. Source alpha must be at least 225, and a 3-by-3 alpha-weighted `(1.5G - 0.5R - B)` mean below -3 identifies the pink fabric; isolated components smaller than eight pixels are discarded. The source-confirmed fabric coverage is restored after skin protection so the generous hand/bag cutouts cannot leave those strips pink. The vector mask's red channel carries general garment coverage and its green channel carries these repairs. Nearby original skin/accessory pixels and transparency are checked against all five blue preview outputs.
